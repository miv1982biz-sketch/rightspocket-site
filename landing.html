Questions

Do you file claims for me?

No — and that’s deliberate. Claims involve statements only you can make about your own property, so you always review and submit yourself. We remove everything around that step: finding the right coverage, the right form, the right documents, and the deadline.

Can’t I just check recalls myself for free?

Yes — saferproducts.gov is free and we link to it. What’s hard is doing it for every product you own, every week, forever, plus tracking warranty dates and card benefit terms that change quietly. That ongoing work is what you pay for.

Do you take a cut of what I recover?

Never. Flat subscription, no success fees. Your recovery is 100% yours.

Which products and cards are covered?

Early access starts with U.S. consumer products (CPSC) and vehicles (NHTSA), and the major U.S. credit cards’ benefit guides. More countries and databases follow — that’s the roadmap, driven by what members ask for.

What do you store about me?

Only your product list and the details needed to prepare claims. No bank logins, no card numbers — you tell us which card you used, never its number. Your list is yours: export it in full at any time, whether or not you ever pay, and it stays exportable if you cancel. Delete everything whenever you like.
